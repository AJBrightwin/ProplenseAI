The problem

Property valuation in India is still done by hand

Every home loan and loan against property needs a valuation. Today that usually means a valuer visiting the site, and that creates four problems.

Slow

Assigning a valuer, visiting and writing a report usually takes several days per file.

Costly

Every site visit has a fee and travel cost, even for a standard flat in a busy area.

Inconsistent

Two valuers can give very different numbers for the same property.

Hard to audit

Under-reported sale deeds and lagging guideline values make the real market hard to see.

Smart routing

Valuers only go where they are needed

Every valuation gets a route based on confidence, property type and the lender's policy.

Green · Desktop decision

High confidence, standard property. No fresh site visit where policy allows.

Amber · Quick remote check

Geo-tagged photos or a video call, reviewed by a valuer at their desk.

Red · Full site visit

Low confidence, high value or unusual property. The valuer gets our report as a starting point.
